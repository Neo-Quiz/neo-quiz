Subject logos, embedded in src/subject-logos.ts (generated from this folder).

Rule (owner, 2026-10-18): only a subject that IS the thing keeps an official
colour logo, i.e. a programming language or a tool. A field never wears one
company's brand and no emoji-style illustration stands in for a subject: those
subjects show a Lucide glyph (dashboard/categorie-affichage.ts). The earlier
brand logos (Intel, Cisco, Spotify, OpenAI, NASA...) and the Fluent UI Emoji /
System Icons illustrations were removed with their licence files.
The logos remain the property of their owners: trademarks are not licensed.
