Subject logos of the subject menu of the Generate page, embedded in
src/subject-logos.ts (generated from this folder). Built 2026-10-10,
updated 2026-10-11.

Rule (owner, 2026-10-10): for each subject, the OFFICIAL colour logo of a
well-known tool or organisation of its field. Owner, 2026-10-11: where a
colour icon of the Fluent UI System Icons set fits better, it is used
instead of a brand logo. Where neither fits, the Fluent UI Emoji
illustration stays (no emoji is added, no Lucide glyph).

by the licence of the files below.
